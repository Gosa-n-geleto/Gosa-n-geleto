### 🚀 Key Work & Highlights

* **Co-Founder & Chief Product Officer @ Chkela (Lolearn Solutions):** Co-built Ethiopia's national matriculation prep platform serving **72,000+ students** with a 90% national pass rate. Engineered ultra-lightweight mobile delivery (compressed to 27 MB) and integrated an Amharic AI tutoring and caching pipeline.
* **Lead Researcher — Off-Grid Edge AI Tutoring Nodes (Addis Ababa University):** Designing *KISA LIFE*, a solar-powered (155W), internet-free classroom server running on an 8GB **Raspberry Pi 5**. Built a local BM25 retrieval and quantized LLM inference pipeline achieving **94.2% syllabus accuracy** completely offline, accompanied by a 5-year deployment cost model.
* **Co-Founder, General Manager & Algorithm Tutor @ ShasheCoders Summer Camp:** Built the application portal (3,000+ applicants, <5% acceptance rate), secured $25k in sponsorship, and led the algorithm curriculum for **150 rural scholars** (scaling to 750+ rural youth via peer-teaching cascades). Recognized as a **Top-5 National Tech Startup** by the Ministry of Innovation.
* **B2B & SaaS Systems Engineering:** Architected LLM-powered Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) for 9+ corporate firms (3,000+ applicants) and built a clinical & volunteer management SaaS platform integrating the Gemini API, OAuth, and WebRTC for 1,500+ users.
